# Stylio v1.1.0

**Status:** release notes draft<br>
**Updated:** October 21, 2026<br>
**Platform:** browser and installable Progressive Web App (PWA)

[Open Stylio](https://wanthinnn.github.io/stylio/) · [Previous release](v1.0.0.md) · [Project README](../README.md)

Changes since Stylio v1.0.0. This update focuses on edited native Styles, third-party HDR
compatibility, and more natural, editable Portrait results.

## Edited native Styles and Texture

- Fix supported iPhone 16/17 photos losing Photographic Styles and falling back
  to traditional Filters after processing an image whose Style was changed in
  Apple Photos.
- Rebuild the missing editing resources in supported Photos Current exports
  using the uploaded photo's selected Style, rather than resetting it to the
  camera's original capture Style. Add Texture/Grain while retaining that
  selection for further editing.
- Improve consistency between the displayed image and the Photos editor on the
  supported reconstruction path, including HDR visibility while editing.
- Keep complete native originals on the existing Texture-only preservation
  path. Their original image, HDR and native editing resources are retained;
  they are not routed through the third-party JPEG colour reconstruction.
- Preserve incomplete or unknown native layouts when their editing resources
  cannot be safely reconstructed. An ordinary SDR thumbnail is no longer used
  as a substitute for the HDR-aware linear resource required by the supported
  reconstruction path.

## HDR from third-party camera apps

- Improve compatible Adobe RGB gain-map JPEG imports, including the tested
  Project Indigo workflow, so HDR remains available in Apple Photos alongside
  Styles, Texture and supported Portrait editing.
- Fix the reported loss of HDR on entering Edit or after changing an adjustment
  and saving, for the validated third-party HDR conversion route.
- Fix HDR colour errors that appeared after Photos edits, especially in bright,
  saturated highlights such as illuminated signs. The accepted route rebuilds
  the base image and HDR gain together instead of simply averaging RGB gain
  channels and losing their colour contribution.
- Regenerate the corresponding display and linear thumbnails for the rebuilt
  HDR image. Keep this correction scoped to fresh RGB HDR JPEG imports;
  existing native HEIC and scalar HDR routes retain their own processing paths.
- Reduce export size by removing unreferenced container data without another
  image encode or a resolution reduction. Referenced image data, metadata and
  editing resources remain intact, including retained inactive HDR resources.
- Bound retained codec memory during the longer HDR conversion to improve
  reliability on large photographs.

## More natural Portrait depth and edges

- Combine available Apple Portrait Effects Matte and skin guidance with IS-Net
  foreground matting and matte-guided EdgeSAM-3x refinement. This improves
  separation of the subject, held objects and background.
- Reduce cases where a bottle's label, keys or parts of a hand are blurred
  despite belonging to the selected foreground. Keep small connected objects
  closer to the subject's focus plane while retaining bounded depth variation.
- Improve thin structures and foreground boundaries, including metal rings,
  occluded fingers and softer skin transitions. Reduce background texture
  incorrectly remaining sharp beside a finger.
- Refine edges on the working depth grid instead of fitting them only on a
  256-pixel thumbnail. Preserve already resolved depth discontinuities and use
  local RGB/depth agreement to guide uncertain transitions.
- Choose an initial focus point inside the accepted foreground instead of
  always starting at the image centre. Manual tap-to-focus remains available;
  Reset restores the suggested initial focus.
- Strengthen the generated Portrait blur response for closer agreement with
  the tested native result at the same aperture.

## Portrait controls, geometry and HDR preservation

- Restore the quick Portrait/ƒ on/off control in the Photos viewer, together
  with editable aperture in Photos Edit, on the validated export layouts.
- Preserve supported native depth and its own camera calibration when reusing
  it. Generated depth instead uses the photo's dimensions and available EXIF
  optics to estimate its projection, with relative image coordinates when
  optics are unavailable.
- Stop applying another capture's lens distortion to generated depth already
  aligned with the source image.
- Preserve native capture metadata when adding Portrait, and match Portrait's
  HDR capacity declaration to the source. This addresses the tested HDR
  dimming and excessive highlight clipping when Portrait is enabled.
- Keep original primary image, HDR, Styles, Texture and source matte resources
  intact on the source-preserving Portrait path. AI mattes guide depth
  internally; they are not injected as new Apple matte resources.

## AI tiers and processing reliability

Standard and Pro now share the same accepted foreground, edge, depth-fusion and
autofocus pipeline. Their only model difference within AI Portrait is Depth
Anything; Pro does not run an additional crop-refinement stage.

| Tier | Depth model | Foreground guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lite | Depth Anything V2 Small Q4 | Available Apple PEM/skin; depth only when guidance is absent |
| Standard | Depth Anything V2 Small FP32 | Apple guidance plus IS-Net and matte-guided EdgeSAM-3x |
| Pro | Depth Anything V3 Small FP32 | Same foreground pipeline as Standard |

- Run depth, matting and boundary models sequentially, releasing workers
  between stages. Verify and cache downloaded model assets for reuse.
- Retain accepted guidance or generated depth when an optional foreground or
  refinement model fails or reaches its own timeout. User cancellation still
  stops the operation.
- Improve source decoding fallback for third-party images so supported inputs
  can still produce depth and Portrait Lighting resources.
- Release result save locks after Portrait success, failure or cancellation,
  while continuing to protect genuinely pending preview-setting updates.
- Add subject-refinement progress feedback and update PWA caching for the new
  foreground modules and model manifest.

## Release notes in Settings

- Tap **App version** in Settings to read the current release notes inside
  Stylio, with grouped sections, formatted lists and readable comparison tables.
- The release sheet follows the app's Light, Dark or System appearance,
  including its app icon. It supports scrolling, swipe dismissal and offline
  reading after the app's cached assets are available.

## Validation and remaining limits

The latest focused regression run passed **285 tests**, with **8 optional
private-fixture cases skipped**. PWA and language consistency checks passed.
A fresh Standard browser export of the accepted key test is byte-for-byte
identical to the device-accepted v6 result. Structural preservation checks cover
source image/HDR resources, editing metadata and their relationships; visual
acceptance still depends on the photograph and Apple Photos version.

- Some reconstructed edited-native Styles photos can retain a small HDR
  brightness difference from the original. Complete native originals use a
  separate preservation route.
- RGB HDR JPEG reconstruction can change the SDR base appearance, take longer
  and produce larger HEIC files because it retains additional HDR resources.
  Container compaction removes unused bytes, not those referenced resources.
- The reported temporary loss of HDR during zoom in Photos is not claimed
  fixed for every third-party source.
- Generated depth and EXIF-based projection are estimates, not measured camera
  depth. Thin details and difficult scenes can still require manual focus or
  produce imperfect separation. A different depth model can change the result.

Keep the original photographs and verify exported copies in Photos. This draft
describes implemented changes; it does not itself announce a production release.
